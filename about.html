Charleston Artist exists because the city's working artists rarely get one place to be found together — the painter in a King Street studio, the aerialist rigging a silk in West Ashley, and the jazz band still playing the tune the city is named for. This page explains how the list gets built, and how to fix or add to it.

The Short Version

One page, every discipline.

Most arts coverage in Charleston is split by discipline — a gallery's own site lists its own painters, a circus troupe's site lists its own performers, a jazz nonprofit lists its own musicians. Charleston Artist pulls all of it onto one wall: visual artists, circus and aerial performers, dancers, and jazz players, alongside the venues, studios, and companies that give them a stage.

It's built and maintained independently, not run by any one gallery, studio, or booking agency listed on it.

Process

How A Listing Gets Made

Every entry is checked against public sources before it goes on the wall.

01
Find

Artists and organizations are sourced from gallery rosters, press coverage, venue listings, and public event pages — never private or unlisted sources.

02
Verify

Each listing is cross-checked against at least one public source — a gallery bio, a news article, or the artist or organization's own site.

03
Catalog

Entries are written plainly: medium or discipline, a one-line description, and a link back to the artist or organization when one exists.

04
Revisit

The directory is updated as artists move studios, companies disband or form, and new names surface — nothing here is meant to be final.
