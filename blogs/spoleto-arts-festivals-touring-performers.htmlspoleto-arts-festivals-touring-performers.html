Spoleto and the festival season that brings touring performers to Charleston

Each spring and fall, opera companies, ballet troupes and jazz headliners from around the world share Charleston stages with the local artists in our Charleston artist directory. This is the calendar to plan around.

Published Nov 17, 2026
6 min read
Charleston, SC

Spoleto Festival USA sets the international standard

Spoleto Festival USA was founded in 1977 by composer Gian Carlo Menotti as the American counterpart to the Festival of Two Worlds in Spoleto, Italy. The 2026 edition ran from May 22 to June 7 with more than 110 performances across opera, dance, theater, music and jazz. The lineup included world and U.S. premieres, and visiting companies included Scottish Ballet.

The festival takes over theaters, churches and outdoor spaces across the peninsula, including the Dock Street Theatre, the Gaillard Center and the Cistern Yard at the College of Charleston. Some productions run through the whole festival and others play a single night, so check individual dates before you build a schedule. Our venue guide covers most of the festival's stages.

Piccolo Spoleto gives local artists the same calendar

Where Spoleto brings in the headliners, Piccolo Spoleto brings out the hometown talent. The City of Charleston's Office of Cultural Affairs runs it on the same dates as Spoleto. The 2026 festival offered 250 events, most produced by local arts and community groups, and half of them were free.

The festival opens with the Sunset Serenade, a free Charleston Symphony Orchestra concert at the U.S. Custom House. The outdoor craft and visual arts show in Marion Square is still the biggest open call for local painters, potters and jewelers each year. Many of the painters and studio artists in our directory show work there.

Charleston Jazz Festival fills nine days each April

The Charleston Jazz Festival ran April 13–21 in 2026 and paired international guests with the Charleston Jazz Orchestra. That year's program included pianist Danilo Pérez playing with the orchestra, guitarist Al Di Meola's acoustic quartet, and tributes to Nina Simone and Art Blakey. Shows are spread across the Music Hall, the Gaillard Center and smaller rooms around town, which gives local players time on the same stages as the touring names in our jazz listings.

North Charleston Arts Festival opens the spring run

The North Charleston Arts Festival leads into Spoleto season with five days of events. The 2026 edition ran April 29 to May 3 with more than 100 national, regional and local artists and performers. It's a good place to catch dancers and visual artists before they move on to the downtown festivals.

MOJA carries festival season into the fall

The MOJA Arts Festival celebrates the arts of the African diaspora through music, theater, dance and visual art. Its 42nd edition ran September 24 to October 4, 2026, and was the first year the festival was run by MOJA Arts, a new nonprofit created to keep the tradition going long term. The festival's juried art exhibition hangs at the City Gallery at Waterfront Park, 34 Prioleau Street.

Planning around festival season

Spoleto and Piccolo Spoleto run together for about two and a half weeks starting in late May, and the city fills up fast. Book lodging early, and plan on one ticketed headliner plus several free Piccolo events each day. Official 2027 dates and lineups will be posted on spoletousa.org and piccolospoleto.com. For the nights between shows, our guide to Charleston nightclubs with live performers lists where performers go after the curtain comes down.
