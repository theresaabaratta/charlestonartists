On The Wall

Painters, Printmakers & Studio Artists

Charleston's visual arts scene runs through King Street's Redux Contemporary Art Center and the working studios that surround it.

01R
Nonprofit Gallery & Studios
Redux Contemporary Art Center

A King Street institution since 2002, Redux houses subsidized studios for 40-plus working artists, the city's only public darkroom, and a rotating exhibition calendar including the annual Creative Corridors studio show.

In Motion

Circus, Aerial & Dance

From West Ashley rig points to the Music Hall stage — Charleston's acrobats and dancers train year-round.

07O
Circus Arts Collective
Out on a Limb Circus Arts

Led by Dr. Andrew Zutell and Dr. Lauren Hernandez-Rubio, this West Ashley-based troupe performs trapeze, aerial silks, lyra, and fire/LED flow at venues from the Charleston Music Hall to Woodlands Nature Reserve.

About This Directory

A running list, not a finished one.

Charleston Artist is an independent directory tracking the people and organizations behind the city's visual art, circus, dance, and jazz scenes. It's updated as new studios, troupes, and performers surface — if you run a Charleston-area arts organization and want a listing corrected or added, reach out.
